# T12 two-tier color evaluation

Protocol: `2.1.0`

- Digital anchors: **50/50 COMPLETE**.
- Uncertainty boundaries: **3/3 COMPLETE**.
- Physical observations: **0/150 run; 150 NOT_RUN**.
- Digital accuracy: **1.0 contract agreement**, not physical-camera accuracy.
- Product rule: show `Không chắc chắn` for margin below 0.10 or poor lighting.

Physical accuracy, lighting stability, and real-world confusion remain unmeasured until consented/licensed captures are supplied.
